INFORMATION FOR GROWN-UPS

Reading and playing together

Read together and let your reader experiment at their own pace. Activities include matching objects, steering, uncovering pictures, adjusting controls, and following sequences. Keyboard and tap controls are provided alongside dragging. You can repeat an activity or turn the page whenever you like.

Use “Read to me” for your browser’s built-in voice. Voice availability varies by device. Sound and scene animation can be switched off independently.

Your chapter is saved only in this browser. This website does not use accounts, advertising, or tracking.
